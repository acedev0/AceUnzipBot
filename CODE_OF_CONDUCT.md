# Community code of conduct

AceUnzipBot should be a useful, safe, and welcoming open-source project.

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, assume good faith, accept constructive
review, protect user privacy, and keep technical disagreements focused on evidence. Harassment,
threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, credential sharing, doxxing, and misuse
of project channels are not accepted.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to protect
the community or project. Report conduct concerns privately to
[Acedevmc@gmail.com](mailto:Acedevmc@gmail.com). Reports will be reviewed discreetly and fairly.
